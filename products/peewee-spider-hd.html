Product Details

The PeeWee Spider HD is our heaviest-duty finesse jig, pairing Jewel's Pro Spider HD head with a custom-specified Heavy Duty Sickle Hook. The weight-forward geometry concentrates mass toward the tip for a sure, clean hook-set — even on heavy line and through thick brush. The hand-tied skirt features a wider color palette than stock jigs, because each one is tied to your exact color match. The weedguard is fiber-matched to the skirt for a clean, natural look that doesn't spook fish.
