Our Story

A small-batch bait brand with big-water standards.

Bass Binge started with one goal: build a jig you trust when every bite matters. Every batch is poured, skirted, and inspected with the mindset of an angler who fishes hard and hates gear failure.

How We Build

We run durable powder finishes, tuned weed guards, and skirt profiles selected for realistic movement. The result is a bait that comes through cover clean and still looks right after a full day of abuse.

How We Test

Each style is tested in shallow cover and deeper structure before release. If it does not track right, hold paint, and convert bites, it does not go in the lineup.

What Matters To Us

Honest gear, clear communication, and local roots. We keep runs tight, improvements continuous, and customer support personal.

Custom Work

Need specific skirt tones, head weights, or hook preferences? Contact us with your target conditions and we will guide the best setup.
